\chapter{Conclusions \& Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\section{What This Work Establishes}
\label{sec:conclusions:contributions}

This dissertation develops an instrument and a decision framework for a problem
that is simultaneously statistical, computational, and cosmological.  The
engineering contribution is a narrow-line detector that uses the ATSC pilot as
a continuously present, spectrally concentrated proxy; combines a fixed tone
projection with local references and robust fine-axis normalization; and carries
the complete decision through exact fixed-point arithmetic.  Its digital
contract is unusually strong: for exercised cases, independent implementations
can be compared by integer equality rather than by a floating tolerance.

The scientific contribution is the refusal to stop at detectability.  The
archive products preserve the statistics needed to sweep masking policies, and
the forecast translates each policy into lost exposure, surviving contamination residual, BAO
parameter bias, and integration-time cost.  Equally important, the
analysis marks where that chain is measured and where it is conditional: the
standard fixes the pilot properties, the archive measures a scalar proxy in one
coarse channel, and additional transfer measurements are required before that
proxy becomes a validated visibility-domain contaminant across an entire 6-MHz
allocation.  Exact implementation evidence cannot substitute for those
measurements.

The principal contributions can therefore be stated without claiming more than
the evidence supports:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item a standards-grounded pilot-frequency model and a nominal
  pilot-to-payload screening relation, separated from the received propagation
  transfer that remains to be measured;
  \item a constraint-derived, exact-integer detector with explicit overflow,
  rounding, initialization, and verification contracts;
  \item a restartable, bounded-storage archive survey --- the
  \texttt{pilot-proxy} archive runtime driving a \texttt{datatrail} extended
  with discovery, inventory, and integrity-checked transfer ---
  that stores exact integer statistics and provenance rather than only
  historical mask bits, so that any coarse or fine policy can be replayed
  exactly on every frame;
  \item empirical era, anchor, null, floor, and correlation-time estimators
  with refusal semantics when a required population is absent;
  \item a synthetic validation path on a bench radio that reaches the full
  $2048$-input geometry by stacking independent captures and measures, rather
  than assumes, the spatial scaling law; and
  \item a two-tier contamination-residual tolerance framework (RFIsher),
  demonstrated for BAO, that selects a per-channel operating point against the
  science tolerance and can return recovery, excision, or measurement-limited
  outcomes while exposing the assumptions responsible for each.
\end{enumerate}

Running through all of it is one arithmetic contract, two receiver profiles, and three evidence platforms.  The
mathematics, weights, and fixed-point contract are stated once, for the
upgraded CHIME correlator; the synthetic evidence comes from GNU~Radio and a
LimeSDR, the archive evidence from CHIME's triggered baseband captures, and
the live evidence from a \texttt{kotekan} stage on the CHORD Pathfinder ---
because no one instrument could supply all three, and because the arithmetic
transfers between them while the calibration does not.

\section{Current Channel-Level Synthesis}
\label{sec:conclusions:matrix}

Under the transmitter proxy, scalar residual chain, thermal-noise-shaped
contamination template, and per-redshift-bin Fisher estimator of
Chapter~\ref{sec:tolerance}, every one of the 23 product-covered channels
receives a current-era screening status and a binding closing condition.  The
status is an analysis status, not an operations command; ``recovery
candidate'' means that the tolerance-selected operating point enters the
dilation-tolerance region on the channel's current era, and it does not waive
the transfer and estimator validations that the closing condition names.

The growth-rate result is more conditional than the dilation result.  In the
present model, booking a BAO-preserving delay cut on both sides of the calculation
can move a threshold-feasible channel inside the $f\sigma_8$ tolerance (Table~\ref{tab:tolerance:worlds}).  The
collaboration-style combined-bin estimator is now implemented over all seven
DTV redshift bins.  In the noise-shaped family its one-year growth-rate
tolerance is $8$--$23\%$ looser than the independent per-bin value, depending
on bin (about $10\%$ at $z=1.4$--$1.5$).  The low-$k_\parallel$, wedge-like,
and localized-$|k|$ banks have also been executed for the exact triplet
per-bin noise-normalized, combined noise-normalized, and combined
fixed-physical; together they span joint-estimator tolerances
of $1.455\times10^{-3}$--$3.795\times10^{-3}$.  This completes the
code-supported estimator and analytic-shape sensitivity envelope, but it does
not select which curve describes the telescope residual.  That selection and
any channel-policy revision require templates fitted to measured visibility
structure.  The
appropriate conclusion is not that the growth rate has already been recovered,
but that a self-consistent filter configuration exists in the screening model
and merits the closing analysis.

\section{What Remains to Close the Verdict}
\label{sec:conclusions:future}

The near program is fixed by the evidence matrix in Appendix~\ref{app:evidence}.
The threshold-free estimator transfer is closed by the fully digital and
controlled radio-path sweeps of Section~\ref{sec:estimator:validation}, the
survey products retain the exact integer terms that make every threshold
replayable, and the Pathfinder stage is implemented and verified against the
offline reference.  The remaining program is:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item complete the LimeSDR detection campaign of
  Section~\ref{sec:detection:synthetic}: the $M$-sweep to $2048$ from stacked
  independent captures, the paired packed-versus-full-precision crossing shifts
  with a stationary-span waveform audit, the fine-versus-coarse gain against
  the coherent-integration bound, and the synthetic mask-versus-residual
  frontier;
  \item complete the archive rerun on all 23 channels and fill every table it
  feeds: stable eras (Table~\ref{tab:calibration:eras}), anchors
  (Table~\ref{tab:calibration:anchors}), current-era nulls
  (Table~\ref{tab:calibration:nulls}), tolerance-selected operating points
  (Table~\ref{tab:tolerance:eta}), the residual chain
  (Table~\ref{tab:tolerance:channels}), the per-channel plates of
  Appendix~\ref{app:archive-diagnostics}, and the cross-build reproduction
  record with its joined denominator;
  \item run the specified cadence campaigns to measure or bound $\tau_c$ on
  the refused channels (half-day sessions at ${\sim}10$-s cadence, the
  large-null, coherence-limited channels first); if they are not executed, the
  sidereal-day caps stand as permanent conservative bounds, and certification
  shifts to the blocked holdout and per-channel jackknife of the final
  analysis;
  \item measure simultaneous power across each 6-MHz allocation to infer the
  distribution of received pilot-to-shelf transfer by channel, era, and
  propagation state;
  \item measure contaminated complex visibilities before and after the
  per-sidereal-day operation, preserving baseline and phase information, and
  separate coherent systematic residual from stochastic added variance;
  \item retain the completed per-bin/joint and four-family analytic forecast
  envelope as the model-sensitivity bound.  Once the visibility products exist,
  fit and propagate frequency-, baseline-, sidereal-, and empirically structured
  residual templates through every channel policy and selected threshold; do
  not choose the most permissive analytic family as a substitute for that
  measurement;
  \item complete the blocked, per-era holdout of
  Section~\ref{sec:survey:holdout} on blocks reserved before the operating
  points were selected, and attach its exact event/frame split and reproduction
  denominator;
  \item run the Pathfinder shadow run of Section~\ref{sec:deployment:results}
  and pass its framing, exact-replay, headroom, monitoring, and
  failure-injection gates; then, for CHIME, pin the upgraded engine's frame
  contract and repeat the alignment demonstration with the CHIME bundles; and
  \item schedule periodic spot-collection on channels excluded from baseband
  collection: transmitter sign-offs and station departures were recorded on
  collected channels during the survey span, and a channel the instrument does
  not sample can never report its own sign-off, leaving recoverable spectrum
  written off indefinitely.
\end{enumerate}

Several forward engineering extensions remain valuable after those gates.  A
third, censored reference could protect the denominator from a discrete line;
alternative reference weights could trade curvature rejection against variance;
and the same exact-arithmetic and calibration architecture could target the
bootstrap or pilot structures of OFDM broadcast standards.  These are extensions
of the detector family, not prerequisites for making the present evidence
honest.

\section{Closing Perspective}
\label{sec:conclusions:closing}

The strongest result of this work is not a promise that every television channel
can be saved.  It is a procedure that makes the answer auditable.  The procedure
starts from a feature the transmitter is required to emit, follows it through a
bit-reproducible digital instrument, records enough information to change the
policy without changing the past, and prices the remaining contamination in the
same units as the cosmological measurement.  Where a digital equality can be
proved, the dissertation requires equality.  Where the telescope can supply a
measurement, it reports the measurement and its population.  Where a transfer
or cadence is missing, it carries a bound or a refusal rather than converting an
assumption into a fact.  That separation is what allows positive and negative
channel verdicts to be scientifically useful rather than merely operational.
