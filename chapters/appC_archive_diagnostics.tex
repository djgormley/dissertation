\chapter{All-Channel Archive Diagnostics}
\label{app:archive-diagnostics}

This appendix places the same diagnostic construction beside every physical
ATSC channel so that a reader does not have to infer the behaviour of any part
of the band from a few selected examples.  The plates are generated by
\texttt{pilot-proxy} from the 23 per-channel
\texttt{pilotproxy\_per\_pilot\_product\_v5} products, on valid frames only,
and they are views of one estimand: the trigger-selected archive.  They are
not samples from a uniform observing calendar, and a blank month in a plate
means that the archive supplied no valid frame in that month; it does not
establish that the television allocation was quiet.

Each plate has three layers, and each layer is one of the objects the body
consumes.

The \emph{upper panels} are the current-era histograms of
Section~\ref{sec:calibration:nulls}: logarithmic-count histograms over the
channel's current-era frames of the coarse power ratio $F/\mu_0$, the same
statistic in the normalized-decibel coordinate, decoded native
complex-\texttt{int4} mean input power, and the fine designated-window
statistic $Z(\rho^\star)$ at the channel's selected rank.  The fitted null
bulk --- median plus left-side scale --- is overlaid on the coarse and fine
histograms, so that the null the threshold was selected against is visible
beside the population it was selected from; the selected $\eta^\star$ is
marked on the fine histogram.  As explained in
Section~\ref{sec:pipeline:decision}, $\mu_0$ is the ratio-of-expected-powers
scale under the declared white/isotropic model, not an assertion that every
finite-sample null distribution has mean one.

The \emph{middle panel} is the masked-versus-unmasked spectrum of
Section~\ref{sec:detection:archive}: the time-averaged spectrum of all
current-era frames and of the frames the selected $(\rho^\star,\eta^\star)$
keeps, each accumulator divided by its own contributing-frame count and
expressed in decibels relative to the median positive bin of the all-frame
spectrum.  It exposes spectral shape, lines, and the change induced by the
mask, but it is neither an absolutely calibrated power spectral density nor a
quantity in watts per hertz.  The dashed orange line is the nominal received
pilot frequency.  A channel on which no current-era frame survives the mask
reports an unavailable after-mask spectrum rather than a zero-power
measurement.

The \emph{lower panel} is the era record of Section~\ref{sec:calibration:eras}:
the arithmetic mean of the fine-stage $F$ statistic in each UTC calendar
month, displayed as $10\log_{10}F$ against fine-envelope frequency.  The dashed
white line is the geometry-predicted pilot anchor; the measured current-era
anchor and the era boundary are marked, and calendar gaps remain visible.  The
panel is deliberately labelled a detector-statistic heatmap: the products
retain one feed-summed power spectrum per frame ($23.8$~Hz bins, $0.01$~dB
codes), which supports any later frame mask and the containment analysis of
Table~\ref{tab:calibration:anchors}, but nothing within a frame and no raw
voltage, so no sub-frame spectrogram exists.  These distinctions are part of
the evidence, not merely plotting qualifications.

Two further panels complete the plate.  An \emph{offset-containment} panel
shows the per-frame fine-peak offset distribution of the current era against
the $K = 64$, $128$, and $256$ spans, the evidence behind the disposition
column of Table~\ref{tab:calibration:anchors}.  An \emph{operating-point}
panel shows the $\eta$ trade curves for the candidate ranks --- masked
fraction against surviving residual, Eq.~\eqref{eq:detection:frontier} ---
with the channel's tolerance and the selected $(\rho^\star,\eta^\star)$
marked, and, where the blocked holdout has been run, the calibration-block and
evaluation-block points with their block-bootstrap intervals side by side.
The middle panel plots $\bar P_{\rm all}$ and the retained conditional mean
$\bar P_{\rm keep|keep}$ of Section~\ref{sec:detection:archive}; the removed
contribution $\bar P_{\rm all} - \bar P_{\rm keep,contrib}$, formed in linear
power with the common denominator $N$, is what the residual chain consumes and
is tabulated rather than plotted.

archive; frames excluded by reason; current era and its valid-frame count;
frames kept by the selected $(\rho^\star,\eta^\star)$; and the plate's file
digest.}{Per-channel denominators for the archive diagnostic
plates.\label{tab:archive:atlas-counts}}

\stub{v5 archive rerun}{Regenerate all 23 plates from the v5 products with
the layers described above.  The plates that follow are the superseded
health-filtered versions: their upper panels show archive-wide (not
current-era) histograms without the null-bulk overlay, their middle panels
show the bootstrap coarse mask rather than the selected fine pair, and their
lower panels mark quarterly anchors rather than the era boundary.  They are
retained so the reader can see the construction and the calendar coverage of
every channel.}
