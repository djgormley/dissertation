\chapter{Evidence, Assumptions, and Closing Tests}
\label{app:evidence}

The dissertation crosses several disciplinary boundaries, and the word
``verified'' does not mean the same thing in each of them.  Table~\ref{tab:evidence:matrix}
centralizes the claim boundary used throughout the revised text.  It is not a
substitute for the derivations in the body; it is a map from each major claim to
the kind of evidence that supports it and to the experiment that would change
its status.  The distinction is intentional: bit equality can close an
implementation question, whereas no amount of bit equality can validate an
astrophysical transfer model.
