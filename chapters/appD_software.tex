\section*{Rerunning it}

The reproducibility claim is operational, not rhetorical: a reader with the
pinned revisions of Table~\ref{tab:software:repos} reruns the chain below and
obtains the same digests.  Each step names the durable check it passes; the
development diagnostics that produced no result in this document are not part
of the chain.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \emph{Detector and kernel} (\texttt{pilot-proxy}): \texttt{make build\-kernel}
then \texttt{make test} runs the release suite of Section~\ref{sec:impl:verify}
--- the golden-vector, oracle, concurrency-emulation, and on-hardware gates;
\texttt{make release-check} adds the specification-document, vocabulary, and profile-contract checks; \texttt{make freeze-check} adds the clean-tree and
commit checks that a release manifest requires.  All must pass before any
product is trusted.
\item \emph{Synthetic campaigns} (\texttt{pilot-proxy}): \texttt{make generate-atsc}, 
\texttt{make audit-atsc}, \texttt{make quantize}, \texttt{make evaluate}, and \texttt{make detect} regenerate the waveform, its stationary-span audit, the
quantized inputs, the estimation sweep, and the detection campaign; their
outputs carry the digests quoted in Chapters~\ref{sec:estimator}
and~\ref{sec:detection}.
\item \emph{Archive survey and products} (\texttt{pilot-proxy} over
\texttt{datatrail}): \texttt{make archive\-integration\-check} confirms the
archive environment; the survey and processing commands of
Chapter~\ref{sec:survey} then produce the v5 products, whose per-unit digests
and the cross-build comparison of Section~\ref{sec:impl:verify} are the
reproduction record.
\item \emph{Calibration, selection, and forecast} (\texttt{RFIsher} with the
\texttt{RadioFisher} fork): the test suite reproduces the clean-survey table
of Chapter~\ref{sec:tolerance} to machine precision, and the selection
workflow emits the per-channel evidence ledger from which every table of
Chapters~\ref{sec:calibration} and~\ref{sec:tolerance} is generated.
\item \emph{Live stage} (\texttt{kotekan} fork): the CI configuration runs the
GPU-oracle and file-pipeline replay tests of Chapter~\ref{sec:deployment}; the
shadow run's acceptance table is evaluated against the recorded run.
\item \emph{This document} (\texttt{dissertation}): \texttt{make verify} rebuilds
the figures from the frozen exports, rebuilds the PDF, and checks every number
against the number gate and the release manifest; \texttt{make manifest}
re-pins the digests after a deliberate change.
\end{enumerate}

The CHIME baseband archive itself is the property of the CHIME collaboration
and is accessed through CANFAR under the collaboration's data policy; the
survey products derived from it are released under the same policy with their
provenance ledgers.  The synthetic captures, the LimeSDR raw recordings, and
the Pathfinder shadow-run records are released with the campaigns that produce
them, with digests recorded in the release manifest.
