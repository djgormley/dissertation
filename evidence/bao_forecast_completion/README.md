# Completed BAO forecast evidence

This directory is the dissertation's immutable copy of the code-side forecast
completion release generated by `WVURAIL/bao-noise-tolerance` commits
`3b5fc5e1a584bd956e8efc69d9a74c359dd905ed` (scientific release) and
`db89d6260428430fd530b2352e1c2bc1cefcab06` (dissertation-font rendering
contract). The producer worktree was clean.
Its complete test suite passed 420 tests, the independent published-number gate
passed all 68 checks, the four complete-v2 evidence files passed their schema,
and the release manifest verified the committed bytes of all 12 listed
artifacts.

## What was completed

The release evaluates all seven DTV-overlapping forecast bins, numbered 5--11,
with both the independent per-bin and collaboration-style combined-bin
estimators. It repeats the combined estimator under the stationary
noise-normalized and fixed-physical-amplitude time families. Four normalized
analytic residual shapes are included: noise-shaped, low parallel wavenumber,
wedge-like, and localized spherical-wavenumber shell. The channel mapping
propagates a forecast-bin result to physical ATSC channels 14--36 only across
bins with non-zero frequency overlap; a boundary channel receives the minimum
accepted tolerance across its overlapping bins.

All 92 analytic-family-by-physical-channel mappings have an accepted common
growth-rate tolerance. This is a model-sensitivity envelope, not an empirical
choice among templates. In particular, the analytic strictness ordering does
not change the dissertation's existing channel-policy status or ranking because
the attachment does not contain the visibility-domain coordinates needed to
infer a physical residual shape.

## Directory map

- `forecast_completion_all_dtv_bins*.json` are the four complete-v2 forecast
  evidence records, including the numerical grids, bank identity, response
  ledgers, estimator outputs, and stability dispositions.
- `forecast_completion_template_comparison.csv` gives the 28 forecast-bin by
  analytic-family comparisons.
- `forecast_completion_channel_mapping.csv` gives the 92 physical-channel by
  analytic-family conservative mappings.
- `forecast_completion_template_status.csv` records both the completed
  analytic families and the explicit empirical-template refusals.
- `forecast_completion_channel_tolerances.{pdf,png}` is the rendered comparison;
  the PDF is also vendored in `figs/fig_bao_template_tolerances.pdf`.
- `forecast_completion_template_summary.tex` and the caption text are compact
  authoring exports; the dissertation prose remains the authoritative
  interpretation.
- `forecast_completion_release_manifest.json` and its JSON schema authenticate
  the portable release. The manifest SHA-256 is
  `862d4c35b5627486cf42399bc3f869f6c0f6dda9dd274bd1d90bcde77a3a037f`.

## Scientific boundary

No new telescope observations enter this release. The frequency-localized,
baseline-localized, and sidereal-coherent empirical template rows remain
`data_dependent_incomplete`. They require contaminated visibility residuals
with authenticated physical-frequency, baseline, and sidereal coordinates and
the corresponding response interface. The dissertation therefore uses the
four analytic families to report sensitivity to a declared set of hypotheses;
it does not select the most permissive family as though it were measured.
